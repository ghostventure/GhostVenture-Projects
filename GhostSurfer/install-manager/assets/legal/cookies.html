Website Cookies

Hosting and Browser Behavior

Your browser and Firebase Hosting may process routine technical request data to deliver pages and downloads. If future analytics, payments, accounts, or support tools are added, this notice should be updated before launch.

Ghost Surfer App Cookies

The desktop app consolidates cookie choices and privacy notices in one non-modal notice panel. You can ask for a decision per site, allow first-party cookies globally, or block all cookies in Ghost Control. Per-site choices can be reviewed and removed in the Cookie Decisions manager. Third-party cookies and sensitive site permissions remain blocked regardless of the first-party cookie setting. The app also provides controls to clear cookies, cache, and history.
