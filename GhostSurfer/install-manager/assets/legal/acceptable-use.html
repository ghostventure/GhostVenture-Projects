Acceptable use

Privacy features have boundaries.

Ghost Surfer may be used only for lawful browsing, research, and ordinary personal or internal business activity.

Prohibited Uses

  • Illegal activity, fraud, identity theft, harassment, stalking, or unlawful surveillance.
  • Malware, phishing, credential theft, spam, bot activity, or unauthorized scraping.
  • Attacks against networks, websites, devices, accounts, or security controls.
  • Infringing copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, privacy rights, or publicity rights.
  • Attempting to bypass lawful restrictions, sanctions, export controls, or court orders.

User Responsibility

You are responsible for the websites you visit, files you download, credentials you enter, networks you use, and local laws that apply to your activity.

Enforcement

Because the current site is static and the browser is local-first, Ghost Surfer does not operate user accounts to suspend. Downloads, pages, or support access may be changed or removed if needed to protect the project, comply with law, or respond to abuse.
