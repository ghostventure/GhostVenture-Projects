Ghost Guard

Privacy controls you do not need a tech degree to use.

Ghost Surfer does not promise invisibility. Windows and Linux include Ghost Guard tracker filtering, sensitive-permission restrictions, and first-party cookie controls. The Mac preview has a separate feature set; each platform's download card lists its requirements and limits.

Permission and tracker controlsOn Windows and Linux, sensitive site permissions are denied by default; known trackers are filtered and alerts are grouped.
Cookie choicesOn Windows and Linux, choose a first-party cookie policy and review or clear saved site decisions.
Optional Tor routingWindows and Linux can use a compatible Tor service already running on your computer; Tor is not included.
Local controlsReview alerts and clear cookies, cache, and history with the app's cleanup tools.

Interface

Everyday controls stay close at hand.

Windows and Linux group tabs, downloads, bookmarks, history, and settings in Ghost Control.

  • Persistent page zoom from 80% to 150%
  • Speed Mode and optional image blocking for heavier pages
  • Keyboard shortcuts for tabs, address focus, reload, and stop loading

Mac · Version 0.2.0

Ghost-Surfer-macOS-0.2.0.pkg

Universal installer for Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 or later. Installs Ghost Surfer.app into Applications. Includes private tabs, WebKit tracking protections, known tracker blocking, HTTPS upgrades, bookmarks, and history. The Mac app uses WebKit cookies and does not include the Windows and Linux apps' per-site cookie controls or local Tor routing.

Published by Black Lion Studios. If macOS asks you to approve the installer, open it once, then choose Apple menu → System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway and confirm Open. The installer package has no Apple Developer ID signature. Existing version 0.1.0 installs need this one-time reinstall to enable future in-app updates.
