Compliance Overview

This page maps the public legal and trust documents included with the Ghost Surfer website.

This is not legal advice. It is a practical compliance checklist for the current static website and free Windows, Linux, and macOS downloads.

Included Documents

  • Privacy Policy covering collection, use, sharing, retention, user choices, privacy rights, children's privacy, and privacy limits.
  • Terms of Use and Acceptable Use rules for lawful use, anti-abuse boundaries, no anonymity guarantee, third-party services, and IP ownership.
  • License Agreement for the current Windows, Linux, and macOS desktop downloads.
  • Cookie Notice for the static website and browser cookie behavior.
  • Refunds and Downloads policy for the current free platform downloads.
  • Security page explaining built-in protections, limits, checksum verification, and issue reporting.
  • Accessibility statement and feedback route.
  • Copyright/IP page and third-party notices.

Current Product Scope

The site provides product information, platform downloads, checksums, and update files. It does not provide user accounts, checkout, subscriptions, advertising pixels, newsletter signup, analytics scripts, browser sync services, or in-site support forms.

Compliance Triggers to Revisit

These policies should be reviewed before adding payments, support forms, analytics, email marketing, account login, cloud sync, crash reporting, telemetry, mobile apps, paid licensing, enterprise deployment, or targeted advertising.

Operational Commitments

  • Keep download checksums current when a release package changes.
  • Keep privacy and security claims specific to implemented behavior.
  • Keep legal pages linked from the primary navigation or footer.
  • Use professional legal review before commercial launch or material data collection.
