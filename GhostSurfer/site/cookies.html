Cookies and Local Storage

The current website is static and does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies.

Website Cookies

The Ghost Surfer website does not currently include account login, analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or cookie banners because it does not intentionally set non-essential cookies.

Hosting and Browser Behavior

Your browser and Firebase Hosting may process routine technical request data to deliver pages and downloads. If future analytics, payments, accounts, or support tools are added, this notice should be updated before launch.

Ghost Surfer App Cookies

The Windows and Linux apps consolidate cookie choices and privacy notices in one non-modal panel. You can choose per site, allow first-party cookies globally, or block cookies in Ghost Control. Review or clear saved site choices in the Cookie Decisions manager. The Mac app uses WebKit cookie handling; see the Mac download details for its feature scope.

Choices

You can use browser controls, Ghost Surfer cleanup tools, or operating system file deletion to remove local browser data.
