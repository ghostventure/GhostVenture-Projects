Privacy Policy

This policy describes data handling for the website and the Windows, Linux, and macOS apps.

This policy is a practical operating document, not legal advice. Final compliance review should be done by a qualified professional if Ghost Surfer becomes a commercial product or collects user accounts, payments, analytics, or support tickets.

Summary

Ghost Surfer provides local-first desktop apps for Windows, Linux, and macOS. The static Firebase Hosting site provides information, checksums, platform downloads, and the macOS update feed. The current website does not include account registration, payment forms, newsletter signup, analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or contact forms.

Information We Collect

The website does not intentionally collect personal information directly from visitors. Firebase Hosting and internet infrastructure may process routine technical information such as IP address, request headers, browser type, URL requested, timestamps, and security logs as part of delivering the site.

The Ghost Surfer apps store browser data locally on the user's computer. On Linux, automatic update checks are enabled by default and contact GitHub's release API at startup. These requests expose an IP address and ordinary connection metadata to GitHub; the updater does not send browsing data. You can disable automatic checks in the app settings. Downloading and applying an update always require confirmation.

On macOS, if you choose “Agree and Enable,” Sparkle periodically contacts the update feed to check for releases and may download signed app updates in the background. The request necessarily exposes an IP address and ordinary connection metadata to the hosting provider. Sparkle's request user-agent includes Ghost Surfer and Sparkle version information; anonymous system profiling is disabled. Sparkle does not send browser history, bookmarks, or page contents. If you decline, automatic checks and downloads stay off. You can enable or disable them from the Ghost Surfer menu. You can still check for updates manually.

Notice at Collection

The current site does not ask visitors to submit personal information. If support email or another contact route is used outside the static website, any information sent by the user may be used to respond to that request, maintain records, and protect the service from abuse.

How Information Is Used

  • To deliver the website and download files.
  • To maintain security, abuse prevention, and hosting reliability.
  • To allow the browser app to remember local bookmarks, settings, and recent browsing state.
  • On Linux, to check for newer releases at startup by default (unless disabled in app settings); downloading and applying an update require confirmation.
  • On macOS, to deliver update checks and signed update downloads when automatic updates are enabled.

Sharing

Ghost Surfer does not sell personal information it receives through its website or apps. Firebase/Google infrastructure serves the website; Linux update checks use GitHub's release API, which receives the connection metadata described above. No third-party analytics or ad networks are intentionally installed on the current site. This does not describe data practices of websites you visit or optional third-party extensions; check their own privacy information.

Retention

Local browser data remains on the user's device until cleared by the user or by Ghost Surfer's cleanup controls. Hosting logs and infrastructure records are handled by the hosting provider according to their operational policies.

Third-Party Browser Extensions

Windows and Linux builds can import optional extensions from local files. Extensions are separate software made by third parties. Depending on their code and permissions, an extension may be able to access information from websites you visit and may send information to its own operator or other services. Ghost Surfer does not audit or endorse imported extensions, and their data handling is governed by their own notices and practices.

Ghost Surfer's cookie choices and site-permission controls do not guarantee that an extension cannot access or transmit data. Ghost Surfer does not define how an extension stores or retains information. Review an extension's source, publisher, requested permissions, and privacy information before enabling it. The Mac preview does not include the desktop extension-import feature.

Your Choices

  • Use Ghost Surfer's clear data controls to remove local cookies, cache, history, saved cookie choices, and Ghost Guard alerts.
  • On Windows and Linux, only import extensions from sources you trust; leave extensions disabled or remove them if you do not need them.
  • Remove the installed app using your platform’s normal process; clearing browser data is a separate action.
  • Do not download or use the software if you do not agree with this policy.

Privacy Rights

Depending on location, users may have rights to know, access, correct, delete, or opt out of certain uses of personal information. The current static website is designed to avoid direct collection, selling, or sharing of personal information. If a rights request applies to information you directly provided through support, email solidartentertainment@gmail.com and describe the request.

Sensitive Personal Information

Do not send sensitive personal information through support unless necessary for the request. Ghost Surfer does not intentionally collect precise location, financial account data, government identifiers, health data, biometric data, or account credentials through the website.

Children

Ghost Surfer is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Privacy Limits

Ghost Surfer blocks browser-level location prompts and reduces common tracking surfaces. It does not guarantee anonymity. Network-level identifiers, including IP address, may still reveal approximate location unless traffic is routed through a VPN, Tor, or similar privacy network.

Contact

For privacy questions, email solidartentertainment@gmail.com.
