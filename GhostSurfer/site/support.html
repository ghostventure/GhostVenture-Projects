Need help?

Here is the simple version: download it, unzip it, and open the app from the folder.

How To Install

  • Download the Ghost Surfer ZIP file.
  • Right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All.
  • Open the extracted Ghost Surfer folder.
  • Double-click Ghost Surfer.exe.
  • Keep the files in that folder together so the app can open correctly.

Checking The Download

The checksum file is there for people who want to verify the download. If you do not know what that means, you can skip it. If a checksum does not match, delete the file and download it again.

Privacy Questions
