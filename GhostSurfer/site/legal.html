Quick guide
  • Ghost Surfer is currently offered as a free download. The site has no account or checkout system.
  • Browser profiles are stored on your device. Website hosting and update checks still involve network requests.
  • Privacy controls reduce some tracking; they do not guarantee anonymity, safety, or compatibility with every website.
  • Features differ by platform. Optional extensions on Windows and Linux are third-party software that you choose and manage.

These summaries help you find the right document; read the linked policy for its full terms. Policies describe the current website and releases, and do not govern external websites or third-party software. This page is product information, not legal advice.
