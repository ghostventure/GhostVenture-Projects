For curious minds

The Reading Room

A short shelf of books for exploring privacy, security, internet history, and the people who shape our digital world.

These are independent reading suggestions, not Ghost Surfer manuals. The browser is a tool; good questions and careful reading are tools too.

Stories from the digital world

Memoir · social engineering

Ghost in the Wires

Kevin D. Mitnick with William L. Simon

A first-person account of deception, computer intrusion, and the security lessons organizations drew from the cases. Read it as a memoir, not as a how-to guide.

Reported nonfiction · cyber conflict

Sandworm

Andy Greenberg

An investigation into destructive cyberattacks and the people who tracked them, told through reporting and the history surrounding the events.

Internet history · communities

The Modem World

Kevin Driscoll

A history of bulletin board systems and the grassroots communities that helped shape online life before the modern social web.

Ideas about privacy and the web

Digital literacy · critical reading

Cyberliteracy

Laura J. Gurak

Examines how to read online with care, including how to recognize persuasion, misinformation, privacy issues, and commercial influence.

Privacy · online reputation

The Future of Reputation

Daniel J. Solove

Explores how gossip, rumor, and personal information spread online, and the tension between expression and privacy.

Technology · policy and everyday life

The Internet in Everything

Laura DeNardis

Looks at what changes when networked technology reaches physical spaces and daily infrastructure, including privacy, safety, and governance.

Publisher pages may change, and some titles may be easier to find through a public library or bookseller. Ghost Surfer does not sell or endorse these books.
