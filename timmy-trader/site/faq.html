Frequently asked questions

Timmy automates configured market and trading workflows. It does not forecast guaranteed returns, remove market risk, or replace the account holder's judgment.

Does Timmy guarantee a return?

No. Timmy applies configured rules to market data and order workflows. Prices, fills, and broker responses can differ from expectations, and trading can result in loss.

How are movement candidates selected?

By default, the saved discovery list requires an absolute daily close-to-close move of at least 1.1%. Automated trade candidates require at least 2.2%, a bullish signal, a scout score of 72 or higher, strategy eligibility, and all applicable account and risk checks. These thresholds are configurable and are not predictions.

What is the difference between Paper, Simulation, and Live?

Paper records simulated activity in its local ledger. Simulation uses a separate local journal and target-aware preview workflow without creating a Webull client. Live can submit broker orders and requires valid account access, exact order preview, and configured safety limits.

Can Timmy trade fractional shares?

Fractional quantities are available only when the selected broker account and instrument support them and Timmy's settings permit their use.

Does Timmy require Webull Desktop?

Timmy connects through Webull OpenAPI for the supported account and order workflows. Webull Desktop is not the connection method; API access, account permissions, and instrument support still apply.

Where are broker credentials stored?

Credentials are entered and verified in Timmy's local Setup workspace. This public website does not collect or store broker keys, account identifiers, passwords, or tokens.

What should I do if an order outcome is unknown?

Check the order and position in Webull and reconcile the order identifier before retrying. An unknown response does not establish that the broker rejected the order; Timmy holds automation until the outcome is resolved.
