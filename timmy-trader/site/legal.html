Legal and risk information

Review the terms, privacy information, and trading-risk disclosures for Timmy and this website. This material is not legal advice.

Risk disclosure

Timmy provides market scanning, order planning, paper and simulation records, and guarded order routing. It does not guarantee returns, prevent losses, or replace your responsibility for your brokerage account.

Automated systems depend on data and assumptions that may not reflect current conditions. Live orders may be rejected, delayed, partially filled, executed at a different price, or result in loss.

Terms of use

Use Timmy only where trading is lawful and only with accounts you own or are authorized to operate. You remain responsible for broker permissions, account funding, trading settings, taxes, and compliance with applicable market rules.

Timmy is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or guaranteed by any broker named in its setup materials. Broker names identify workflows that may be compatible with the application.

Privacy policy

This website does not collect broker credentials. The contact form opens a draft in your local email application; messages are not submitted to or stored by this site. Enter credentials in the desktop application and keep them under your control.

Do not send account identifiers, access tokens, passwords, application secrets, or private trading records through public support channels.

Security notes

Verify the package checksum before installation. Keep broker keys out of source control, screenshots, and support messages. Revoke broker access if you believe credentials were disclosed.

Accessibility

The public pages use semantic headings, keyboard-accessible links, visible form labels, and responsive layouts to support access across common devices and input methods.
