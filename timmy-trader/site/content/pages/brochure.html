02 / WORKFLOW

Timmy makes each stage of its workflow inspectable. It can scan markets and prepare plans; the operator configures accounts, selects an execution lane, and controls whether an order is submitted.

1

Set market and execution preferences in the desktop application. Broker credentials remain in the local profile.

2

Screen and review

Examine candidate symbols, data quality, strategy context, and the reasons a plan is ready or blocked.

3

Assess the proposed order

Review entry, stop, target, quantity, account state, and configured exposure limits before proceeding.

4

Select an execution lane

Paper and Simulation record local outcomes. Live requires account verification, broker permissions, market eligibility, and explicit safety settings.

Operator authority

Three lanes. Distinct records.

03 / SAFETY & FIT
Paper · local ledger

Record simulated orders and outcomes in the local paper ledger. These records are not broker-confirmed positions or returns.

Simulation · scenario record

Evaluate proposed plans through local preview and submission controls. Outcomes use daily candles and do not model broker fills or market microstructure.

Live · broker orders

Submit real orders only after account verification, exact order preview, configured exposure and drawdown limits, and all applicable Timmy and broker checks.

Automation remains inspectable. Timmy organizes market information and applies configured checks. It cannot assess every aspect of an operator's financial circumstances or guarantee an outcome.
