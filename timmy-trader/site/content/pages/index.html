Finance-Punk market system · Linux

Independent markets. Disciplined execution.

Timmy is a Linux market-scanning and trade-assistance platform. It monitors configured symbols, evaluates rule-based candidates, and keeps strategy, account, and order controls visible throughout the workflow.

Automation, with an audit trail. Market discovery, position sizing, account checks, and order status remain visible in one locally operated workflow.

A measured path from discovery to review

Timmy brings market data, strategy rules, account readiness, and execution status into a single desktop workflow.

Identify market activity

Rotating universe scans rank daily price movement against configurable percentage thresholds. Discovery remains separate from the active manual watchlist.

Evaluate risk before sizing

Plans apply configured quantity, notional, account-cash, and strategy limits. Fractional quantities depend on broker and instrument support.

Maintain a reviewable record

Separate paper, simulation, and execution journals preserve decisions and broker outcomes for follow-up.

Designed for operator oversight

Local configuration, explicit account checks, and distinct execution lanes make each stage of the workflow easier to inspect.

Credentials remain local

Broker credentials are entered in Timmy's Setup workspace. The public website does not request or store them.

Live execution is gated

Live orders require a verified account, supported instrument, current account data, exact order preview, and configured risk limits.
