Frequently asked questions

Timmy turns configured market signals into a reviewable workflow, with explicit gates between discovery, a trade plan, and an order. Automation can organize decisions; it cannot promise their outcome.

Does Timmy guarantee a return?

No. Signals and scenario records are not forecasts of profit. Market data can be delayed or incomplete, prices can move, broker orders can be rejected or filled differently than expected, and trading can result in loss. You remain responsible for account settings and every live-trading decision.

How does movement discovery work?

The default discovery threshold saves symbols whose absolute daily close-to-close move is at least 1.1%. It ranks movers by the size of that move and keeps a bounded list. This is a daily observation, not a continuous intraday alert, and changing the threshold changes which symbols qualify.

When does a mover become an automated trade candidate?

A symbol must meet the default 2.2% movement threshold, have a bullish signal and a scout score of at least 72, and pass strategy eligibility. It must then clear the normal symbol, account, market, order, and risk gates. A movement threshold or shortlist ranking alone never submits an order; the thresholds can be configured.

What does the mover scan cover?

The configured source universe includes US-listed stocks and ETFs, selected benchmarks and proxies, and limited crypto and futures symbols. It does not cover every global listing, individual bond, option contract, or instrument. Daily bars and provider availability shape what the scan can find; check the data timestamp and source status in the app.

How do Paper, Simulation, and Live differ?

Paper records simulated orders, cash, and positions in a local ledger using loaded candle prices; it does not send orders to Webull. Simulation has a separate local journal and a target-aware preview/submit flow, without constructing a Webull client. Its daily-candle scenarios assume the entry price and do not model intraday priority, fills, slippage, fees, or broker behavior. Live can place real broker orders and requires verified account access, fresh account checks, supported instruments, risk limits, and an exact successful order preview.

What keeps Live automation from placing an order?

Live automation requires explicit Live Auto opt-in and valid positive exposure and drawdown limits. Timmy also checks the current account snapshot, open positions, pending priced buy orders, session, candle quality, cooldowns, order caps, and other configured risk controls. Any unmet or uncertain gate can hold or reduce an order. Keep the preview and review the blockers shown in the app.

What can Timmy do through Webull?

Timmy uses Webull OpenAPI for supported account checks, buying-power refresh, order previews and supported order submissions, plus watchlist sync where available. API approval, account permissions, region, instrument eligibility, and broker-side availability constrain these workflows. Webull Desktop is not the connection method. Timmy does not move money between accounts.

Can Timmy trade fractional shares or every asset type?

Only where the connected account, instrument, API, and Timmy settings all support the requested quantity. Support is not universal. Options, direct indexes, forex, CFDs, and other unsupported instruments do not have a complete Live execution path; crypto and futures may remain disabled or scouting-only unless their specific support and permissions are validated.

Where are broker credentials stored?

Enter and verify Webull credentials in the desktop app's local Setup workspace. The public website does not receive them. The application stores its verified profile locally on the computer; protect that computer and do not put keys, tokens, passwords, account identifiers, or MFA codes in support requests, screenshots, or source control.

What should I do if an order outcome is unknown?

Do not retry just because a request timed out. Check Webull's order and position state, reconcile the client order ID, and resolve the notice in Timmy. A timeout or server error does not prove that Webull rejected the order. Timmy holds automation for an unresolved outcome so a duplicate is not sent while the first order is uncertain.

Where can I get help?

Use the support guide to check the installed version, account readiness, market-data timestamp, risk blockers, and unresolved order notices. When contacting support, include the version, workspace, expected behavior, and exact error text. Remove credentials, account identifiers, and private trading records.
