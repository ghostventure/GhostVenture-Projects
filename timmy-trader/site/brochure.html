02 / WORKFLOW

Timmy is built to make each step visible. It can scan and prepare plans, but users stay in control of account setup, lane selection, and whether an order is ever submitted.

1

Set up locally

Configure market preferences in the desktop app. Webull credentials, if used, are entered locally and are not sent to this website.

2

Scan and inspect

Review active symbols, setup context, data quality, and the reasons a plan may be ready, watch-only, or blocked.

3

Check the plan

Inspect the proposed entry, stop, target, quantity, and configured exposure limits before proceeding.

4

Choose a lane

Use Paper to record simulated outcomes. Live remains gated by account checks, broker permissions, market rules, and explicit settings.

◌

Market scanning

⌁

Risk-aware plans

Applies configured quantity, notional, per-trade, and account-cash limits.

▤

⌖

⌘

03 / SAFETY & FIT
Paper · Simulated

Practice the workflow and review simulated results in Timmy's local journal. Simulated outcomes may differ from real execution and do not establish what a live account would have earned.

Live · Broker guarded

Live order placement is disabled by default. It requires explicit settings, a verified supported account path, buying power, market eligibility, and the relevant Timmy and broker gates.

Designed for review, not blind trust. Timmy can organize market information and apply configured checks. It does not know your complete financial circumstances and cannot guarantee a result.
