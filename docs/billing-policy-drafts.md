# Billing, Trial, Cancellation, and License Policy Drafts

Status: product-policy drafts for legal review before live launch. They are not jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

## OverHead Team Identity and Authorization

An internal OverHead Team account must use an authorized `@overheadteam.com` or `@overheadteam.biz` email address. An authorized supervisor, manager, or administrator must approve and issue the employee license before access is active; the authorization record retains the approving account and issue time. This internal-team policy does not apply to a customer business's own workspace staff, who may use that business's approved company email domain.

## Subscription Billing and Authorization

Paid plans renew on the interval shown at checkout unless canceled. Before payment, the workspace administrator must review the tier, price, billing interval, and recurring-billing authorization. OverHead blocks duplicate checkout for an already-active tier and holds a short checkout lock. Stripe is the payment processor and authoritative transaction record.

## Free Trial

Each workspace may receive one free, card-free 30-day Gold-equivalent trial. The trial starts at activation and ends exactly 30 consecutive days later. It is not transferable, extendable, redeemable for cash, or refundable. Creating multiple workspaces or accounts to receive additional trials is prohibited.

## Cylinder Evaluation and Activation

Cylinder is a separate Windows Server/Windows IoT deployment and is not sold through the standard desktop checkout. Each server workspace may receive one card-free 30-day evaluation. The evaluation creates no payment and does not automatically renew. Continued use requires a separate deployment order or contract, billing confirmation, and an issued Cylinder entitlement. Before payment, provide the final price, billing interval, renewal, cancellation, refund, support, and entitlement terms. These terms remain drafts for qualified legal review before commercial launch.

## Cancellation and Prorated Refunds

An authorized workspace administrator may cancel an eligible paid subscription from OverHead. Cancellation ends access immediately. OverHead calculates unused time within the current paid billing period and requests a proportional refund to the original payment method. A free trial, fully used period, unpaid amount, failed payment, duplicate request, or time outside the current paid period has no refund due. Stripe and the card issuer determine settlement timing.

## Workspace Licenses

OverHead automatically assigns internal licenses to active workspace members based on the current entitlement. Licenses are not transferable and may be suspended for trial expiration, cancellation, payment failure, account suspension, or acceptable-use violations.
