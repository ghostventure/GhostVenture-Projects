# OverHead business principles and operating standard

Status: product and communications standard. Legal drafts still require qualified counsel review before a commercial launch.

## Mission

Make daily office operations clearer, more accountable, and within reach for owner-led businesses.

## Product scope

OverHead is a local-first Windows workspace for customer follow-through, revenue operations, role-aware approvals, and support-ready records. It is designed to complement a business's payment providers, professional advisers, security practices, backups, and industry-specific compliance program. It is not a substitute for professional judgment or required compliance work.

## Operating principles

1. **Clarity before clutter.** Every workflow should make the next action, responsible role, current status, and handoff visible.
2. **Owner control.** Material changes to money, customer outcomes, access, recovery, or policy should be explainable and routed for review when the business needs it.
3. **Minimum necessary information.** Collect, display, export, and send only the information required for the task. Never send secrets or unredacted records to support.
4. **Progressive adoption.** Start with one useful workflow and expand only after it is helping the business.
5. **Evidence and recovery.** Keep understandable records of important actions, provide local backup/export tools, and validate recovery rather than assuming it.
6. **Straight dealing.** State what is live, what needs configuration, what a connected provider controls, and what remains planned. Do not imply certification, traction, revenue, or billing availability that has not been verified.

## Required workflow pattern

1. Capture only the context and permission needed.
2. Assign a responsible role and identify the review threshold.
3. Take the action or create the next task.
4. Retain the decision and handoff context needed to understand the result.
5. Use privacy, backup, support, and recovery controls when the issue or action warrants them.

## Legal and commercial alignment

Public and in-product policy language must reflect the current product state. Terms, privacy, access/security/recovery, AI/automation, payment-provider, support/evidence, fair-use, and launch/change-management disclosures are drafts for counsel review. A production payment, renewal, cancellation, trial, or refund promise may be published only after the corresponding backend/provider flow is operational and verified.

## Shared license policy

Each active workspace member has one internal license record. The record is linked to the member's Firebase user ID and workspace, not to a browser, a device, or a transferable code. The workspace entitlement determines the license tier and status; the member's workspace role determines what they may do. Members may view only their own license record. Workspace administrators may review the workspace license register. License and subscription identifiers are retained by OverHead for support and entitlement resolution; customers do not need to manage or share them. A pending or suspended license must be displayed plainly and must not be represented as active access.

## Research basis

This standard is informed by practical small-business guidance from NIST and the FTC: access control and least privilege, data minimization, strong authentication, incident/recovery planning, and clear safeguards. It is not a claim that OverHead is certified by, compliant with, or endorsed by either organization.
