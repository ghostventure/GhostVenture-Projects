# OverHead 0.3.28

This feature wave adds faster operational workflows for customer progression, field dispatch, inventory control, team time approval, payroll preparation, business health review, and integration setup readiness.

- Customer Journey provides a searchable internal board from lead through follow-up, with customer and estimate references.
- Field Operations provides a date-based dispatch queue with local assignment, work status, queue order, and site notes.
- Inventory & Reorder tracks SKUs, locations, on-hand quantities, reorder points, stock movements, and CSV export.
- Time Approvals reviews submitted time entries and exports approved hours for payroll preparation. It does not submit payroll or calculate wages or tax.
- Business Insights summarizes available operational records and highlights immediate follow-up work without presenting the data as accounting-grade profit.
- Integration Readiness tracks setup steps for accounting, calendars, customer communications, APIs, and cloud files. Checklist completion does not indicate a live provider connection.

Several workflows store their records in the encrypted local workspace. They do not add live GPS routing, customer payment processing, public booking, provider synchronization, or payroll submission.
