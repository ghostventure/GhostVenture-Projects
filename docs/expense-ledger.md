# Expense ledger

The Expense Ledger is available on the Team Dashboard to users with the **Owner** or **Bookkeeper** role. Both roles can record expenses, review and change expense statuses, and set monthly category budgets. Other roles do not see the ledger.

## Record and review expenses

Enter a description, vendor, category, amount, expense date, and optional notes, then select **Submit expense**. New entries start as **Pending**. The ledger shows records for the selected month, including each expense's description, vendor, category, date, amount, and status.

The available statuses are **Pending**, **Approved**, **Rejected**, and **Paid**. Mark an expense **Approved** before marking it **Paid**. A rejected expense must be returned to **Pending** before it can be approved. These statuses track review and payment progress; the ledger does not send a payment to the vendor.

## Set monthly category budgets

Choose a month, select a category, enter its budget amount, and select **Save category budget**. Budgets are set separately for each month and category. The budget summary compares recorded spending with the budget for each category and shows the remaining amount or overspend. Rejected expenses are excluded from actual spending; other statuses count toward the monthly actual.

Categories are Advertising, Insurance, Meals, Office supplies, Payroll, Rent, Software, Travel, Utilities, and Other.

## Local data handling

Expense records and budgets are saved in the current local OverHead workspace. Status changes and budget updates are also added to the local audit history. They are not sent to a vendor or accounting service by the expense ledger. Treat expense notes and local workspace backups as business records, and protect any exported or backed-up files accordingly.
