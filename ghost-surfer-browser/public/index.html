Ghost Guard

Privacy controls you do not need a tech degree to use.

Ghost Surfer does not promise magic invisibility. It gives you clear, practical privacy controls: websites ask before keeping cookies, common trackers get blocked, risky permission requests are stopped, and Ghost Guard tells you when something tried to cross the line.

Stops Location RequestsWebsites are blocked from asking the browser for your exact location by default.
Cookie Approval By SiteWhen a site wants cookies, Ghost Surfer asks. Approve to retain cookies, or decline to block them for that site.
Spy AlertsIf a known tracker or blocked permission request appears, Ghost Guard warns you immediately.
Optional Tor SupportIf you already use Tor on your computer, Ghost Surfer can use that route when it is available.
Locks Down Nosy PermissionsCamera, microphone, notifications, screen sharing, clipboard, and local font access are blocked by default.
Future Console LookA sharper 2033-style privacy console with neon edge lighting, dark glass panels, and 12 theme-aware accent colors.
Tamper WarningIf local browser settings are changed outside the app, Ghost Surfer warns you before you keep browsing.
Startpage HomeThe browser opens with Startpage so search starts cleaner from the first tab.
HTTPS PreferredGhost Surfer upgrades plain HTTP pages to HTTPS when the secure version is available.

Interface

Not another Chrome clone.

Ghost Surfer puts the important stuff close by: privacy status, cookie decisions, spy alerts, speed tools, downloads, bookmarks, history, themes, and cleanup. You do not have to hunt through layers of menus just to change the basics.

  • Signal-style address bar with quick Launch, Speed, and Grid controls
  • 12 theme-aware accent colors for the 2033 privacy console look
  • Control center for privacy, cookie decisions, history, downloads, bookmarks, and tabs
  • Speed Mode and optional image blocking for heavier pages
  • One-click cleanup for cookies, cache, and history
