      <section id="privacy" class="section shell">
        <div class="section-heading">
          <p class="eyebrow">Ghost Guard</p>
          <h2>Privacy controls you do not need a tech degree to use.</h2>
          <p>
            Ghost Surfer does not promise magic invisibility. It gives you clear, practical privacy controls:
            websites ask before keeping cookies, common trackers get blocked, risky permission requests are
            stopped, and Ghost Guard tells you when something tried to cross the line.
          </p>
        </div>
        <div class="feature-grid">
          <article class="feature"><strong>Stops Location Requests</strong><span>Websites are blocked from asking the browser for your exact location by default.</span></article>
          <article class="feature"><strong>Cookie Approval By Site</strong><span>When a site wants cookies, Ghost Surfer asks. Approve to retain cookies, or decline to block them for that site.</span></article>
          <article class="feature"><strong>Spy Alerts</strong><span>If a known tracker or blocked permission request appears, Ghost Guard warns you immediately.</span></article>
          <article class="feature"><strong>Optional Tor Support</strong><span>If you already use Tor on your computer, Ghost Surfer can use that route when it is available.</span></article>
          <article class="feature"><strong>Locks Down Nosy Permissions</strong><span>Camera, microphone, notifications, screen sharing, clipboard, and local font access are blocked by default.</span></article>
          <article class="feature"><strong>Future Console Look</strong><span>A sharper 2033-style privacy console with neon edge lighting, dark glass panels, and 12 theme-aware accent colors.</span></article>
          <article class="feature"><strong>Tamper Warning</strong><span>If local browser settings are changed outside the app, Ghost Surfer warns you before you keep browsing.</span></article>
          <article class="feature"><strong>Startpage Home</strong><span>The browser opens with Startpage so search starts cleaner from the first tab.</span></article>
          <article class="feature"><strong>HTTPS Preferred</strong><span>Ghost Surfer upgrades plain HTTP pages to HTTPS when the secure version is available.</span></article>
        </div>
      </section>
