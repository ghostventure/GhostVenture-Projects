Fast answers for new starters

The next six cards share proactive guidance most users need before asking a question.

Account creation

Create an account, verify email if prompted, and choose the Host or Performer workspace that fits what you need to do.

Profile accuracy

Keep your role, location, availability, portfolio, venue details, and contact information current so matching stays useful.

Booking fees

CLTCH may show platform fees, processing fees, or optional paid features where those tools are active. In-product pricing controls the workflow.

Payout workflow

If CLTCH provides an active payment path, the displayed terms apply. If payment is direct between users, those users handle that relationship.

Local compliance

Hosts, venues, and performers remain responsible for permits, insurance, age rules, licensing, taxes, and event-specific requirements.

Reporting issues

Flag safety, payment, copyright, privacy, accessibility, or suspicious booking concerns through support or legal@cltch.ntwrk.

Marketing & visibility

Complete your performer page, add useful tags, and respond professionally so your profile gives hosts a clearer reason to book.

Host checklists

Use venue details, setup notes, age restrictions, permits, and safety requirements to make gig posts clearer before talent responds.

Guided onboarding

Start with the role workspace, profile fields, Terms, Privacy, Compliance, and support paths so your account is set up cleanly.

Safety first

Each booking should keep event contacts, location, setup requirements, support paths, and safety concerns easy to find.
