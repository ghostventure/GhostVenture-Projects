About CLTCH.NTWRK

The network built for live creative bookings.

CLTCH.NTWRK connects hosts and venues with musicians, DJs, performers, photographers, cinematographers, models, and other creative talent. Post a gig, match with the right people, manage the booking, and keep the operational pieces connected.

Why we built this

Booking live talent has always been a manual, disconnected process — cold DMs, outdated referral lists, and missed opportunities. Performers spend more time chasing leads than performing. Hosts spend more time on hold than on the floor.

We built CLTCH.NTWRK to fix that. A real-time platform where gig postings surface to matched, available talent nearby, where booking status is visible, and where support, compliance, privacy, accessibility, and trust workflows are part of the product instead of afterthoughts.

How it works

For Hosts

1

Create a venue profile

Add venue details, contact info, booking preferences, and operational requirements so talent can understand the job before responding.

2

Post a gig

Specify the date, time, category, style, pay range, location, age restrictions, setup notes, and what kind of talent you need.

3

Get matched and confirmed

Relevant talent can see opportunities that fit their role, location, availability, and profile signals. Booking state keeps both sides aligned.

4

Rate the performance

After the gig, leave a grade and short feedback. Ratings help great performers get more opportunities.

For Performers

1

Build your performer profile

Add your display name, category, genres or styles, portfolio, setup, rate, availability, and service area so the matching engine can find fitting gigs.

2

See gigs matched to you

Open requests that fit your profile, availability, location, and preferences appear in your workspace with less noise.

3

Swipe or tap to accept

Review the details, accept when the fit is right, and keep track of upcoming booking status from the workspace.

4

Build your reputation

Every gig you complete earns a grade from the host. Your grade history is visible on your public profile.

What we handle vs. what you handle

✅

CLTCH.NTWRK handles

  • Location-based matching
  • Real-time gig feed
  • Availability calendars
  • Performer ratings & grades
  • Profile visibility
  • Booking status and workspace routing
  • Support, trust, and compliance escalation paths
🤝

You handle directly

  • Final payment obligations unless CLTCH expressly provides an active payment-processing feature
  • Event contracts, riders, employment classification, or union agreements
  • Equipment and logistics
  • Permits and licensing
  • Any required permits, licenses, insurance, supervision, or safety staffing
