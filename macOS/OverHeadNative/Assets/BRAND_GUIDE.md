# OverHead logo

The arch is the overhead structure that keeps the office organized. The three linked rows represent customer context, repeatable workflows, and the decisions that keep work moving. Violet adds focus; mint marks progress.

## Assets

- `OverHead-Logo.svg` — horizontal wordmark for light backgrounds.
- `OverHead-Logo-Reverse.svg` — horizontal wordmark for dark backgrounds.
- `OverHead-Mark.svg` — app, Dock, and profile mark.
- `website-next/public/assets/overhead-social-card.svg` and `.png` — Facebook and Open Graph link preview.
- `website-next/public/assets/overhead-facebook-cover.svg` and `.png` — Facebook page cover image at 1640 × 624.
