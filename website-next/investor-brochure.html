OHOverHead

Investor brochure

A focused operating layer for the local business office.

OverHead brings customer context, active work, approvals, payment handoffs, support evidence, and owner visibility into a practical local-first desktop workflow.

Mission: Make daily office operations clearer, more accountable, and within reach for owner-led businesses.

Opportunity

Small business administration is still full of expensive handoffs.

Customer context is often split between notes, inboxes, payment tabs, staff conversations, and the owner's memory. OverHead is built to return that operating layer to one clear command surface.

The entry point is narrow by design: solve the recurring work that happens after the customer calls, then earn the right to deepen the workflow.

Investment lens

Start where workflow repetition is already painful, visible, and worth organizing. A focused product can earn adoption without asking a small office to buy a broad software suite upfront.

Buyer

Owner-led service businesses

The buyer feels the daily cost of scattered follow-up and unclear ownership.

Entry point

Installed office command center

A focused Windows workspace for records, queues, approvals, payments, and evidence.

Commercial model

Tiered subscription

Silver, Gold, and Black create a path from owner control to higher-volume operations.

Product proof

Real desktop surfaces, not concept-only mockups.

OverHead Desktop smoke-test capture: signed-in command console with workspace status, customer database, current work, office readiness, and security indicators.

Business standard

Clarity, owner control, and straight dealing.

Make the next action visible, keep sensitive decisions explainable, and state product and commercial boundaries plainly.

Product thesis

Operational memory compounds.

Business rules, records, approval history, and support context become more useful as the workflow becomes habitual—when customers choose to build on proven use.

Strategy

Win the office routine, strengthen trust, then widen the system.

01

Earn the daily workflow

Start with customer context, queues, approvals, payment handoffs, and support evidence that an owner feels every day.

02

Increase trust with the account layer

Move from local workspace identity toward secure shared accounts, verified access, and disciplined administration.

03

Expand from proof

Add integrations and vertical workflow packs where they directly improve throughput, retention, or customer confidence.

Scope

Practical operations—not an enterprise-suite promise.

A local-first Windows workspace for customer follow-through, revenue operations, role-aware approvals, and support-ready records.

Next

Validate before expanding.

Use a narrow pilot to measure activation, repeat use, conversion, and retention before deepening account administration or provider integrations.
