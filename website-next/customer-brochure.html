OHOverHead

Business office guide

Bring the daily office into clearer view.

OverHead is a local-first Windows workspace that helps an owner-led business organize customer follow-through, active work, approvals, and support-ready records.

See the next action, keep the right context, and retain control of sensitive decisions without taking on an enterprise-sized system.

What it helps with

One practical workspace for the work that tends to scatter.

Customer follow-through

Keep useful context close.

Organize customer details, service notes, preferences, and the next step so staff do not have to reconstruct the story.

Daily operations

Make the next action visible.

Use current-work queues, reminders, workflows, quotes, invoices, and follow-ups to keep routine handoffs moving.

Owner control

Keep decisions explainable.

Use roles, approvals, activity records, exports, and recovery tools to keep important changes understandable.

Example desktop workspace. Available functions depend on the installed version and any connected provider configuration.

How to start

Start with one workflow that matters.

1. Set up the owner workspace

Give the office a clear home.

Create the owner profile, set the business context, and choose the role each person actually needs.

2. Choose a first use

Prove value in daily work.

Begin with customer follow-through, a quote review, a reminder queue, or another workflow that already causes friction.

3. Review material change

Keep people accountable.

Use management approvals where a change affects a customer, money, access, policy, or recovery state.

4. Keep a recovery path

Prepare for the unexpected.

Use local backup, export, support-bundle, and restore-validation tools—and protect the files they create.

What to expect

Clear boundaries support better decisions.

OverHead is designed to organize

  • Customer context and active work
  • Quotes, invoices, approvals, and handoffs
  • Role-aware access and support-ready records
  • Local backup, export, and recovery guidance

OverHead does not replace

  • Owner judgment or staff supervision
  • Professional legal, accounting, or security advice
  • A payment provider's processing or dispute process
  • Your required compliance, backup, or incident plan
Privacy and support

Use only the customer information needed for the work. When asking for help, share the app version, what happened, and a sanitized support bundle when useful—never a password, recovery phrase, full card detail, tax identifier, or unredacted customer record.
