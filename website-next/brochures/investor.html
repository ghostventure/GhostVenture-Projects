OHOverHeadInvestor overview · September 2026

Software for the small business office

Build around the work that happens every day.

OverHead brings customer records, tasks, quotes, invoices, and approvals into an installed Windows workspace.

The target buyer is the owner of a small service business: close to the customer, responsible for the team, and still carrying too much of the office in their head.

THE PROBLEM

Scattered context.

Notes, inboxes, and separate tools make routine follow-up harder to track.

THE ENTRY POINT

One recurring job.

Start with customer follow-through and expand as teams use the workspace.

THE MODEL

Tiered access.

Silver, Gold, and Black support increasing team and workflow needs.

The adoption thesis

Win a useful daily routine first. Measure whether teams return, complete work, and choose to keep using the product before expanding the scope.

Commercial validation plan

Measure adoption
before widening the product.

01 / TARGET

Choose one office segment.

Recruit a small group with a repeated customer follow-up problem. Define the buyer, current tools, and switching friction.

02 / ACTIVATE

Get to the first useful result.

Measure account setup, the first customer record, and the first completed workflow. Record where onboarding stalls.

03 / RETAIN

Watch repeat use.

Track weekly active workspaces, completed work, support demand, and why teams continue or stop.

04 / CONVERT

Test willingness to pay.

After billing is enabled and verified, measure trial-to-paid conversion, renewal, and the cost of supporting each account.

Priorities that support that plan

Complete payment-service setup, establish trusted Windows signing, simplify onboarding, and run a focused pilot. Expand integrations only where pilot evidence supports the work.
