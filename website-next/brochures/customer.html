Each Desktop tier includes one user; added seats halve the previous seat price (rounded down) to a $14.99 minimum. Desktop charges cap at Cylinder's $499 per month, and Cylinder is recommended when the uncapped total would pass $499. The first paid month is free at checkout and does not stack with the existing trial. Confirm current annual and trial terms before purchasing.
